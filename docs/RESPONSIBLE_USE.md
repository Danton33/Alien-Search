# Responsible use

Alien Search is an unofficial research tool. These rules keep users, the people who share it and the public sites it reads out of trouble.

## With government websites

- Read only what is public. The tool never logs in, submits forms or tries to get past "Access denied", CAPTCHA or "verify you are human" pages, and you should not either.
- Respect robots.txt and the pacing built into the tool. Do not remove them, run many copies in parallel, or wrap the tool in proxy, rotating-address or "unblocking" services.
- Large downloads (release bundles, bulk archives) can be gigabytes. Check the size first and download only what you will actually review.
- If a site blocks automated access, use an alternate official copy, an archived copy, or download the file in your own browser and import it.

## With documents and findings

- Never present the tool, its outputs or your research as official, or suggest a government agency endorses it.
- Label where every document came from: official site, official alternate, archive (with capture time), user-supplied or secondary mirror. A matching SHA-256 fingerprint shows two files are identical; it does not show their contents are true.
- Keep claims, agency findings and your own interpretation separate. Unresolved does not mean extraterrestrial.
- U.S. federal government works are generally public domain, but records can include third-party material (newspaper clippings, letters, photos), and other countries' records can carry reuse terms. Check before republishing documents in bulk. Linking to the official source is always fine.

## With private information

- Keep the state folder, downloads, API keys and the local API token private. Never paste keys or tokens into a chat, issue or screenshot.
- Records sometimes name private individuals such as witnesses. Do not collect, publish or combine personal details about them beyond what your research question needs.
- Before publishing, follow docs/PUBLISHING.md to keep your own identity and other people's private details out of the repository.
